{} About Us

A community-based, artist-centered organization.

Over the last decade, Versatile Image has evolved — from helping businesses craft their stories through an artistic, social-impact-driven lens, to becoming something rooted even closer to home: a nonprofit dedicated to equipping creative professionals and entrepreneurs with the tools to build real, sustainable income.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between cultural expression and economic opportunity, particularly within underrepresented communities.

{} Our Programming

Three tracks. One mission.

Equipping creatives with the tools, strategy and infrastructure to monetize their art form, and reinvesting it in the communities they come from.

01.

Track 01

Business & Operational Infrastructure

The business foundation every creative needs but rarely gets: practical, step-by-step instruction in the mechanics of running a business, grounded in the lived experience of the communities we serve.

  • Business registration & legal structure
  • Pricing strategy for creatives
  • Contracts, invoicing & getting paid
Format
Workshops · 1:1 technical assistance · Cohorts
Who it’s for
Creative professionals, entrepreneurs & small business owners
Learn More about Business & Operational Infrastructure (opens in a new tab)

02.

Track 02

Creative Technical Skills

Talent is not enough. Professional-grade technical training, taught by working professionals, so creatives can charge what they’re worth.

  • Graphic & digital design: Adobe, Canva, Figma
  • Photography, lighting & photo editing
  • Video production & social content
Format
Workshops led by working professionals
Who it’s for
Creative professionals seeking discipline-specific training
Learn More about Creative Technical Skills (opens in a new tab)

03.

Track 03

Community Open Workshops

Free and open to all. Hosted by our partner artists, who are paid to teach their craft, sharing skills that are usually gatekept by cost, location or social capital.

  • Smartphone photography & social content
  • Painting, drawing & community murals
  • Music, beat-making & spoken word
Format
Free, drop-in sessions
Who it’s for
Everyone in the community
Learn More about Community Open Workshops (opens in a new tab)
